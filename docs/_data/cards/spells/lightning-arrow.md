---
name: Lightning Arrow
castingTime: "Bonus Action, which you take immediately after hitting or missing a target with a ranged attack using a weapon"
range: Self
level: 3
school: Transmutation
components: "V, S"
duration: "Instantaneous"
---

As your attack hits or misses the target, the weapon or ammunition you’re using transforms into a lightning bolt. {Instead of taking any damage or other effects from the attack, the target takes 4d8 Lightning damage on a hit or half as much damage on a miss.|The target takes 3d8 Lightning damage.} {Each creature within 10 feet of the target then makes a Dexterity saving throw, taking 2d8 Lightning damage on a failed save or half as much damage on a successful one.|Each creature within 10 feet of the target takes 1d8 Lightning damage.}

The weapon or ammunition then returns to its normal form.

**_Using a Higher-Level Spell Slot._** The damage for both effects of the spell increases by 1d8 for each spell slot level above 3.
